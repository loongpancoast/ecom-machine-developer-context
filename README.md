# Ecom Machine

## Project brief

Build a complete, private, cloud-hosted autonomous ecommerce operating system that runs multiple brands from one application: product research, creative production, storefronts, advertising, suppliers and post-purchase operations. The full original business vision is the commissioned delivery, not a smaller agreed core, pilot or future out-of-contract target.

The system must turn research into finished ads and pages, launch live product tests, collect results and improve. Its database connects every product, source, brief, script, prompt, asset, page, campaign, cost, order and decision. Owner approval establishes the initial creative direction and operating permissions; routine work then runs autonomously within approved budgets and controls.

Creative quality is central. Outputs must reflect credible brand references, real customer language and product-specific evidence, not generic AI copy, unfinished generations or template visuals. Actual decoded ads and functioning pages must be visually and functionally reviewed against references, followed by an iteration grounded in measured results.

### Complete commissioned scope

- Product and competitor research, customer-language analysis, economics, offers and live product testing.
- Briefs, reference transcripts, scripts, image ads and finished UGC/avatar/video ads, including editing, captions, audio, transitions and exports.
- Complete Shopify store creation: domains, branding, native Liquid, responsive navigation, policies, checkout, payments and shipping configuration.
- Catalog creation and bulk management: products, variants, collections, media, copy, pricing, metafields, templates, imports and store migration, with stable IDs and duplicate-safe updates.
- Conversion-led landing/product pages, bundles, upsells, cross-sells and post-purchase offers, plus SEO/AEO/GEO implementation and testing.
- Meta Facebook Page creation/configuration, Instagram linkage where applicable, business/ad-account connections, catalogs, Pixel and CAPI, with verified purchase events, consent and deduplication.
- Advertising, spend/results tracking, analytics, heatmaps, controlled tests and iterative optimization.
- Private supplier discovery, quotes, outreach, negotiation, diligence, purchasing and quality control.
- Dropshipping, bulk/private-label inventory, order routing, warehouse/3PL fulfillment connections, shipment tracking, returns and support.
- Retention, bookkeeping/accounting connections, landed costs, cash planning and profitability analysis.

These are connected business functions to deliver, not a requirement to own a physical warehouse, recreate payment rails or build an unrelated standalone ERP. Platform permissions, legal/KYC checks and owner approvals must be identified and supported; they do not remove the capability from scope. See [requirements and acceptance](REQUIREMENTS.md) for the complete delivery contract.

### 60 live product tests per month

**60 LIVE PRODUCT TESTS PER MONTH is both an operating requirement and a final acceptance requirement.** Each qualifying test needs real research and unit economics, an offer, finished ads, a functioning purchase destination, a live budgeted campaign, verified tracking and recorded spend, results and decisions. Research rows, creative variants, simulations, test-mode orders and repeated entries are not additional live product tests.

Before hiring, agree the monthly measurement window, launch cadence, per-test observation windows, budget allocation and result/stop criteria. Final acceptance requires the documented monthly live-test run, not a projected capacity benchmark. Ad/media/platform and other operating spend is separately approved from the engineering fee. The aspiration of 2-3 scalable winners and a majority reaching break-even through iteration is not a guaranteed commercial outcome; that uncertainty does not waive the 60-test delivery requirement.

### Owned capability, not a bundle of SaaS links

Own the interface, source code, database, workflows, source evidence, assets and business history. Amboras, Arcads, Higgsfield, Atria, Brandsearch, WinningHunter and Kalodata illustrate capabilities to create inside this engine, not a requirement to buy each subscription or literally reproduce every vendor feature. Use supported integrations and proven components when useful, while retaining workflow ownership and a replacement path.

The engine must research top ads and landing pages, analyze hooks, pacing, narrative, CTA and angles, then produce original product-specific finished ads and pages, launch them, measure and improve. Respect access and media rights; do not copy unauthorized footage or invent testimonials, reviews or claims. Keep first-party measured outcomes separate from competitor/vendor estimates and marketing claims.

The visual research surface shows product images, scraped landing pages, competitor ads, prices, source links/dates and concise assessments, and connects selected products to execution. A dashboard is a review surface, not the system itself.

### Budget and delivery

Budget: $6,000-$8,000 fixed fee, negotiable. $8,000 is the ceiling, not an automatic or committed fee. The target is roughly one month, with required access ready. Agree final price, milestones, schedule, measurement windows and operating spend before hiring. Milestones stage delivery of the complete system; they do not reduce scope. The schedule must accommodate monthly live-test acceptance and disclose external approval dependencies.

**A demo, scaffold, disconnected automation or partial build is unacceptable as final delivery.** Initial applicant samples and walkthroughs are evidence for recruiting, not substitutes for final acceptance. Research, creative, stores, campaigns and business operations must work together in the deployed engine.

Codex AND Claude must power the deployed engine as well as development, through officially supported access. Verify runtime terms, automation permissions, capacity and recovery when limits are reached; never assume subscriptions include API usage. Direct image AND video APIs are preferred. Use an aggregator such as Higgsfield only where its skill or complete workflow improves the rendered result, through an adapter within the owned creative engine. Disclose and obtain approval for operating costs; this repository authorizes no spending.

We are engineers who use Codex, Claude and vibe coding. Unsupported padded manual-from-scratch estimates are unacceptable. Explain custom work, reuse, integrations, creative finishing, testing and deployment without inventing coding-hour savings or pretending testing disappears. Honest technical pushback is welcome. Acceptance fixes and the upgrade demonstration are included; extra paid work requires prior written scope and price approval.

## Future-proofing

The engine must keep up with the AI race, newer models, better agents and increasingly AGI-like capabilities. This means demonstrated adaptability, not a promise of literal AGI, permanent immunity to obsolescence or lifetime maintenance. Replace models, providers, skills and whole workflows, and combine or remove obsolete stages without losing business context, history, source, data or assets. Keep permissions and durable records independent of prompts. Compare actual output quality, cost, speed and reliability, version changes and demonstrate restoration. A model dropdown alone is insufficient. See the [upgrade demonstration](methodologies/agent-led-execution.md#upgrade-demonstration).
