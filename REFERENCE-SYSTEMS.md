# Reference systems

These services are capability and output-quality references, not a required architecture or proof of an integrated application.

## amboris — likely Amboras

The owner supplied the spelling **amboris**. [Amboras](https://www.amboras.com/) is a close match to the described storefront system, but that identity still needs confirmation.

Amboras describes AI-driven storefront construction and editing, product and order operations, and autonomous A/B optimization. Its [agentic ecommerce overview](https://www.amboras.com/what-is-agentic-ecommerce) describes a CRO beta for selected stores with existing traction. Do not assume the optimization feature is generally available to every new store.

**Useful reference:** turn business/product context into a functioning storefront, then use measured behavior to test improvements.

**Integration unknowns:** a suitable public API, embedding rights, complete data export, automation coverage and commercial access are not established by these pages. Website functionality alone does not prove backend integration is available.

## arcads — Arcads

[Arcads](https://www.arcads.ai/) markets avatar/video-ad generation, editing and a workflow canvas. It is an ad-production reference, not an autonomous storefront/page-builder reference.

Its [AI video API page](https://www.arcads.ai/features/ai-video-api) and [API documentation](https://external-api.arcads.ai/docs) provide an integration starting point. The [terms](https://www.arcads.ai/terms) indicate API access requires a Customized offer; confirm the applicable plan and contract rather than assuming a standard subscription includes API use.

**Useful reference:** produce usable avatar/video ads and variants from scripts and product context, with editing as part of the production workflow.

**Integration unknowns:** authenticated job creation, completion handling, asset export, rate limits, pricing, usage rights and access to specific UI features must be established before adoption.

## Developer evaluation

- Compare finished ads and pages against the intended brand quality, not marketing claims or demo metrics.
- Establish supported API operations, authentication, scopes, asynchronous jobs, webhooks or polling, quotas and failure handling.
- Confirm commercial terms, generated-asset rights, export formats and a replacement path.
- Keep the application's interface, database, version history, costs and decisions independent of the service.

The descriptions above reflect official product pages, not authenticated API or generation tests. See [requirements](REQUIREMENTS.md) for the full scope.

## Ad intelligence and reference-led production

The [capability and workflow guide](methodologies/ad-intelligence-and-reference-led-creation.md) connects earlier research methods with current official descriptions:

- **WinningHunter:** product/ad discovery, competitor matching, store tracking and research data.
- **Kalodata:** TikTok Shop product, creator, video and livestream relationships; a current documented API, not the older no-API assumption.
- **Atria:** ad libraries, creative analytics, performance-informed briefs, image variants and feedback into the next creative.
- **Brandsearch:** brand/product/ad/landing-page research, Spectre monitoring and reference-led ad recreation.
- **Higgsfield:** ready-made creative skills, Ad Reference/Recreate, UGC and other production workflows, plus documented website-building capabilities.

These are capability references, not five mandatory subscriptions or verified integrations. Learning from our ad results is required; a claim of continual model training is not assumed. See the guide for source links, evidence limits, access/cost checks and the reference-to-output demonstration.
