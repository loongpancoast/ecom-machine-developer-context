# Reference systems

These services are capability and output-quality references for capabilities to CREATE inside the owner-controlled, multi-brand private-cloud autonomous ecommerce engine. They are not a required architecture, a bundle of SaaS links, or proof of an integrated application. The commissioned deliverable is the completed full system, not a smaller core with the rest left as a future vision; reproducing every proprietary vendor feature or dataset is not required.

## Amboras

[Amboras](https://www.amboras.com/) is the storefront capability reference named in the current brief. Earlier source material used the spelling **amboris**.

Amboras describes AI-driven storefront construction and editing, product and order operations, and autonomous A/B optimization. Its [agentic ecommerce overview](https://www.amboras.com/what-is-agentic-ecommerce) describes a CRO beta for selected stores with existing traction. Do not assume the optimization feature is generally available to every new store.

**Useful reference:** turn business/product context into a functioning storefront, then use measured behavior to test improvements.

**Integration unknowns:** a suitable public API, embedding rights, complete data export, automation coverage and commercial access are not established by these pages. Website functionality alone does not prove backend integration is available.

## arcads — Arcads

[Arcads](https://www.arcads.ai/) markets avatar/video-ad generation, editing and a workflow canvas. It is an ad-production reference, not an autonomous storefront/page-builder reference.

Its [AI video API page](https://www.arcads.ai/features/ai-video-api) and [API documentation](https://external-api.arcads.ai/docs) provide an integration starting point. The [terms](https://www.arcads.ai/terms) indicate API access requires a Customized offer; confirm the applicable plan and contract rather than assuming a standard subscription includes API use.

**Useful reference:** produce usable avatar/video ads and variants from scripts and product context, with editing as part of the production workflow.

**Integration unknowns:** authenticated job creation, completion handling, asset export, rate limits, pricing, usage rights and access to specific UI features must be established before adoption.

## Developer evaluation

- Compare actually decoded finished ads and rendered, functional Shopify stores/pages against the selected references and intended brand quality, not marketing claims or demo metrics. Inspect the purchase journey, record defects and produce the next measured iteration.
- Establish supported API operations, authentication, scopes, asynchronous jobs, webhooks or polling, quotas and failure handling.
- Confirm commercial terms, generated-asset rights, export formats and a replacement path.
- Keep the application's interface, database, version history, costs and decisions independent of the service.

## Commissioned-system boundary

- Deliver full Shopify stores with domains, branding, native Liquid, responsive navigation, policies, payment/shipping setup and working checkout. Include duplicate-safe bulk catalog creation, import/migration and management of products, variants, collections, media, copy, prices, metafields and templates; bundles, upsells, cross-sells, post-purchase flows and SEO/AEO/GEO tests belong in the system.
- Connect Meta business/ad accounts, Facebook Pages/configuration, Instagram links as applicable, catalogs, Pixel/CAPI, consent and browser/server deduplication; verify purchase events against actual test orders.
- Include connected sourcing, quotations, supplier diligence and negotiation, sample/QC, purchasing, order routing, 3PL fulfillment/tracking, returns, support, retention, accounting and profitability. This is integration and operation, not construction of a proprietary warehouse or payment rail. Owner/KYC/legal approvals are dependencies to resolve, not scope exclusions.
- Operate **60 live product tests per month** as an operating and final-acceptance requirement. Each has completed research/economics/offer, finished ads, a functioning checkout destination, a live budgeted campaign, verified tracking, and recorded spend, results and decisions. Candidate rows, creative variants, simulations and replays do not count. Preagree measurement windows and obtain separate operating-spend approval; the target never authorizes unapproved spend.
- Use **Codex AND Claude** for development and the deployed engine through verified official supported access, capacity and runtime terms. Prefer direct image AND video APIs; use an aggregator only where its complete workflow/skill improves the rendered result, behind an owned replaceable adapter. There is no video-only paid-API cap; subscriptions, APIs, media, ads and other operating costs still require approval.
- Demonstrate safe replacement of models, providers, skills and whole workflows, including combining/removing obsolete stages. Compare actual output quality, cost, speed and reliability; retain data, business context, history and source assets with versions and restore. This is how the engine keeps up with newer models, better agents and increasingly AGI-like capabilities, not a literal AGI-immunity or lifetime-maintenance promise.

Initial work samples and bounded demonstrations help evaluate the developer; they are not final acceptance. A scaffold, demo, disconnected automation or partial build is unacceptable final delivery. Milestones stage the complete delivery rather than reduce its scope. The $6,000-$8,000 negotiable range has an $8,000 ceiling, not an automatic fee, with roughly one month as the target; agree final price, schedule and milestones before hiring. Engineers are expected to use Codex, Claude and AI-assisted/vibe coding efficiently. Honest technical pushback is welcome; unsupported padded manual-development estimates are not.

The vendor descriptions in the Amboras and Arcads sections reflect official product pages, not authenticated API or generation tests. The commissioned-system boundary states current owner requirements, not vendor-verified delivery. See [requirements](REQUIREMENTS.md) for the full scope.

## Ad intelligence and reference-led production

The [capability and workflow guide](methodologies/ad-intelligence-and-reference-led-creation.md) connects earlier research methods with current official descriptions:

- **WinningHunter:** product/ad discovery, competitor matching, store tracking and research data.
- **Kalodata:** TikTok Shop product, creator, video and livestream relationships; a current documented API, not the older no-API assumption.
- **Atria:** ad libraries, creative analytics, performance-informed briefs, image variants and feedback into the next creative.
- **Brandsearch:** brand/product/ad/landing-page research, Spectre monitoring and reference-led ad recreation.
- **Higgsfield:** ready-made creative skills, Ad Reference/Recreate, UGC and other production workflows, plus documented website-building capabilities.

These are capability references to implement inside the owned engine, not five mandatory subscriptions, a link bundle or verified integrations. Connect permitted ad/page scraping and analysis of hooks, pacing, narrative, CTA and angles to ORIGINAL product-specific finished ads/pages, launch, measurement and improvement. Learning from our ad results is required; a claim of continual model training is not assumed. See the guide for source links, evidence limits, access/cost checks and the reference-to-output demonstration.
