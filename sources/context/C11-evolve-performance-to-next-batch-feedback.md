## Inputs

- Performance data from Agent 10
- Ad creative references
- Growth Guide

---

## Process

### Step 1: Classify Results

For each ad set tested, categorize into:
- **Losers:** identify WHERE in the funnel they failed
- **Winners:** identify WHY they won

---

### Step 2: Diagnose Losers

| Symptom                        | Diagnosis                                                                 | Fix                                           |
|--------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Low Hook Rate                  | The hook failed. The concept might be fine.                               | Fix the hook.                                 |
| Low Hold Rate + Good Hook      | The body/middle lost them. Hook works.                                    | Rewrite the hold section.                     |
| High Engagement + Low CVR      | The ad entertains but doesn't convert.                                    | Make more conversion-driven. Add stronger CTA, proof, risk reversal. |
| No Spend at All                | Meta didn't find this ad relevant to anyone. Major miss on audience/angle.| Rethink angle entirely.                       |
| Low CTR                        | Copy/creative isn't compelling enough to click. Weak offer or weak CTA.   | Strengthen offer and CTA.                     |

---

### Step 3: Analyze Winners

For each winner, document:
- What desire did this ad tap into?
- What sub-avatar was targeted?
- What format/style was used?
- What was the hook type?
- What mechanism was introduced?
- What made this feel native/authentic?
- Pull the **FRAMEWORK** from the winner (the structural pattern that made it work)

---

### Step 4: Form Hypotheses

Use the format:

> "If I [specific change], then [expected outcome], because [evidence from data]"

### Step 5: Design Next Batch

Apply the **Iteration Methodology:**

| Scenario                              | Next Batch Type           | Description                                                                                      |
|---------------------------------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Current batch was initial test         | **Big Swings (N+1)**      | Broad exploration of new concepts, angles, formats                                               |
| A winning element was found            | **Medium Swings (N+1)**   | Dig deeper into WHY the winning element worked                                                   |
| Medium swings showed promise           | **Deep Research (N+1)**   | Go back to Reddit/Amazon/TikTok for new angles on the winning element                            |

---

### Step 6: Update Growth Guide

- Mark completed batches with results
- Add new batch entries with hypotheses
- Update cumulative learning log

---

### Step 7: Identify Patterns Across Batches

Answer these questions when 3+ batches have been completed:

- Which desires consistently perform?
- Which formats get the most spend?
- Which hook types have highest hook rate?
- Which sub-avatars convert best?

Use these patterns to refine the avatar and angle strategy.

---

## Output

- **Learnings Report** (per batch): what worked, what didn't, why
- **Hypothesis Set** (minimum 3 testable hypotheses)
- **Next Batch Design:**
  - Control ad (keep winner running)
  - Test variant (specific change)
  - Success metric
  - Kill threshold
- Updated Growth Guide
- Cumulative Learning Log
- Pattern analysis (if 3+ batches completed)
