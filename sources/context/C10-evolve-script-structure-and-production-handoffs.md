## Process

### VIDEO ADS

#### Step 1: Select Angle from Growth Guide

Choose the angle that aligns with the current DCT batch and avatar segment.

#### Step 2: Write 3 HOOKS (0-3 seconds each) per concept

Each hook must stop the RIGHT person scrolling. Label who each hook is for.

**Hook Rules:**
- Use VOC language from research — the words customers actually use
- Each hook must clearly identify and speak to a specific person
- Must work in the first 1-2 seconds of the scroll

#### Step 3: Write BRIDGE for each hook (3-10 seconds)

- Connects hook to body
- Introduces the mechanism that makes your product different
- Makes it tangible and relatable

#### Step 4: Write 1 HOLD (10-60+ seconds) — the body copy

Follow this structure in order:

1. **Agitate failed solutions:** "You've tried [solution 1], [solution 2]..."
2. **Explain WHY they fail:** "They only [partial fix]. None of them [real fix]."
3. **Introduce YOUR mechanism:** "[Product] is the first/only [category] with [mechanism]"
4. **Clinical/social proof:** "In a [study/survey], [specific result]"
5. **Paint the outcome:** "Imagine [dream state]"
6. **Risk reversal:** "Try it for [X] days 100% risk-free"
7. **CTA:** "Try it risk free for less than $[price]/day"

#### Step 5: Include VISUAL DIRECTION notes

For each section (hook, bridge, hold), write what the editor should show on screen. Examples:
- Close-up of product
- B-roll of frustrated person at scale
- Text overlay with stat
- Customer testimonial clip

### STATIC ADS

#### Step 1: Image-First Approach

Describe the visual style first, then write copy to match.

#### Step 2: 6-Part Copy Structure (long-form image ads)

1. **Identity Hook** — who this is for
2. **Story/Problem** — relatable scenario
3. **Failed Solutions Bridge** — what they've tried
4. **Mechanism Introduction** — why this is different
5. **Proof/Results** — social proof, clinical data
6. **Offer + Close** — CTA with risk reversal

#### Step 3: Short-Form Variants

- Headline + subhead + CTA for template-based statics
