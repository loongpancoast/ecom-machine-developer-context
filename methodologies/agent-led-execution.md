# Current direction: agent-led execution, deterministic foundations

This direction supersedes historical static automation diagrams, fixed prompt chains, vendor pins and implementation recipes elsewhere in the archive. Retain old material only for a concrete method, data relationship or failure lesson. Do not use its age alone to reject useful evidence, and do not treat its presence as a stack requirement.

## What to build

Build a custom agent-led ecommerce operating system. Agents research, compare evidence, select tools, form plans, inspect results, revise creative work and recover from failures. The system should adapt to the product, audience, evidence and observed outcome rather than force every case through a fixed sequence of automation nodes.

Use Codex and Claude subscriptions through supported access for the core engine; paid APIs are limited to video generation. Capability and usage limits must be demonstrated, not assumed. The full-system delivery contract remains unchanged.

## What stays deterministic

Reliable software still handles database transactions, schema validation, permissions, budgets, signed requests, idempotent writes, queue state, bounded retries, job polling, media rendering and telemetry. These are tools and safeguards for agents. Having an LLM invent arithmetic, repeatedly submit a paid job or guess whether a transaction succeeded is not an improvement over tested code.

Agents decide what to do next within authority. Deterministic services enforce what may happen and record what actually happened. Each external effect requires readback; uncertainty must not silently trigger duplicate spending, orders or publication.

## Keep node-based creative methods, not legacy orchestration baggage

A node view should expose the current plan, dependencies, references, alternative generations, chosen takes and edit lineage. Support intervention and regeneration of affected branches. Do not require n8n, Zapier, a frozen DAG or a single prewired prompt chain as the operating brain. A repeatable subroutine is acceptable where it is the reliable way to execute an agent-selected operation.

## Keep, summarize or retire

- **Keep:** source-linked research methods; course-derived reasoning; reference-video analysis; before/after frame-pair controls; AI UGC A-roll/B-roll; product/identity consistency; editing; measured feedback.
- **Keep as supporting primitives:** useful data contracts, schemas and small tool wrappers. Historical templates are not production-ready merely because they are executable text.
- **Summarize:** old automation patterns that teach useful input/output, asynchronous-job, provenance or failure-handling lessons.
- **Retire from the active handoff:** stale runnable exports, placeholder-heavy automation skeletons and obsolete platform setup recipes that add no method beyond the summary. Preserve originals outside the current handoff or in history, with a dated removal record.

## Acceptance

Demonstrate a real end-to-end task and a meaningful correction: new evidence, a missing source, a failed tool or a rejected creative should change the next action appropriately. Preserve inputs, outputs, decisions, costs and artifacts. A chatbot response, static workflow diagram or successful mock is not the operating system. Autonomous does not mean unrestricted authority, unlimited retries or unbounded spend.
