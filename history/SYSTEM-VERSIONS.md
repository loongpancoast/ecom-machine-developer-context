## Important distinctions

- May dropship revisions, June Python pipelines, July Research OS and September Ecom Machine packages are distinct artifacts, not aliases for the latest Business OS. Same archive basenames do not establish identical content.
- The brain's June documentation archive is not a complete executable backup. The separate earlier vault retains implementation candidates that need file-level review before public export.
- The bundled Research OS connector references an engine that is not included. Engine/CLI dependency closure remains outstanding.
- Business OS simulator-backed workflow tests do not establish live commerce integrations. The broader portfolio-commerce specification exceeds the narrower catalog implementation.
- Installed ComfyUI templates and historical Pletor notes are references, not evidence that a business workflow rendered successfully. Native Flora history remains unresolved.
- These source families were not re-run as part of packaging. Source, configuration, test reports, actual external effects and business results remain separate evidence levels.

## Coverage limits and privacy

Branch and stash bodies, nested packages, unnamed attempts and the full course-transcript corpus have not been exhaustively reviewed. Whole source applications and third-party course/prompt bodies are not bundled. Public methodology summaries preserve attribution; private product tests, customer/supplier records, interviews, credentials and original local paths remain excluded.
