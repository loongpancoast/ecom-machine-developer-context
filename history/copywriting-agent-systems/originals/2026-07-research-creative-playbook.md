# Research-to-creative operations playbook

## Output

A source-linked research packet that compiles into an offer, message hierarchy, page brief, and creative test matrix without losing provenance.

## Operating sequence

### 1. Research brief

Define the product, market, customer, decision to support, evidence standard, time window, jurisdictions, exclusions, and required deliverables.

### 2. Evidence collection

- Collect current first-party product facts, customer language, reviews, support data, competitor positioning, ads, pages, offers, and market context.
- Preserve URL or file provenance, capture date, source type, and confidence.
- Separate verbatim customer language from interpretation and agent inference.
- Respect access, copyright, privacy, and platform restrictions.

### 3. Synthesis

Compile:

- dominant desires, problems, outcomes, and awareness levels;
- repeated phrases, objections, anxieties, failed alternatives, and proof demands;
- product mechanism, unique mechanism or positioning hypothesis, benefits, differentiators, and limitations;
- offer components, risk reversal, friction, and economics;
- contradictions, missing evidence, and claims that cannot ship.

### 4. Message and offer architecture

Choose one audience, one primary desire, one promise, one mechanism, one proof strategy, and one offer hypothesis per test. Tie every claim to the evidence packet or label it a hypothesis.

### 5. Creative translation

- Build a matrix of angles × hooks × formats × proof types × awareness levels.
- Write briefs with concept, opening, narrative, demonstration, CTA, required assets, forbidden claims, and success measure.
- Repurpose winners by changing one dimension deliberately; preserve the winning mechanism.
- Keep brand naming and visual directions traceable to the brand system.

### 6. Production and QA

Verify source rights, product accuracy, claim support, spelling, captions, safe zones, aspect ratio, duration, destination congruence, and rendered output. Record asset lineage and final IDs.

### 7. Learning loop

Join platform performance with creative taxonomy and business economics. Record what the result supports, what it does not support, and the next falsifiable hypothesis.
