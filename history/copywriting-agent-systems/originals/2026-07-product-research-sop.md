# Product research R0-to-R3 gate SOP

## Non-negotiable rules

- No product advances on an unsupported score or unattributed claim.
- Record verbatim evidence, inference, contradiction, missing data, and confidence separately.
- Recheck supplier, price, shipping, availability, ads, competition, and policy at the gate where the decision is made.
- A gate packet is immutable evidence; corrections append a dated revision.

## R0 — idea intake

Create a unique product record with source, capture date, product description, customer job, market, initial signal, owner, and known risks. Deduplicate against the live registry before opening work.

Exit only when the idea is identifiable, traceable, not already active under another name, and worth a bounded feasibility screen.

## R1 — feasibility screen

Verify:

- demand evidence and customer language;
- supplier availability, variant consistency, lead time, shipping, landed-cost inputs, and sample path;
- current selling prices, competitive density, differentiation space, and active advertising evidence;
- platform, claim, safety, intellectual-property, refund, and fulfillment risks;
- provisional unit economics with unknowns exposed.

Disposition: `advance`, `hold-for-evidence`, `duplicate`, or `kill`, with reason and evidence.

## R2 — dossier

Build a source-linked dossier covering market, customer, desire, awareness, competitors, offers, creatives, reviews, objections, mechanism, proof, supplier, economics, operations, compliance, and test plan. Resolve material contradictions or escalate them explicitly.

## R3 — build-ready packet

The handoff must contain:

- canonical product facts and selected supplier/variant;
- current economics and sensitivity cases;
- audience, positioning, offer, proof, claim limits, and message hierarchy;
- asset and sample requirements;
- storefront, creative, tracking, fulfillment, support, compliance, and rollback requirements;
- experiment budget owner, kill conditions, and success measures;
- unresolved risks and next verification date.

No downstream build starts from chat history alone.
