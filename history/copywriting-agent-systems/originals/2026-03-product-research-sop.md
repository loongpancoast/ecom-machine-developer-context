## GATE R0 — IDEA INTAKE

**Goal:** Capture enough proof to justify deeper work.

**Inputs (pick at least 2):**
- TikTok Creative Center (Top Ads)
- Meta Ad Library (live competitor ads)
- Amazon/Google best seller signals
- Google Trends (demand stability check)

**Definition of Done:**
- [ ] Product concept (one sentence)
- [ ] Desired outcome (what buyer wants, not features)
- [ ] 3 competitor links (ads or stores)
- [ ] Compliance risk: Low / Med / High (and why)
- [ ] Route hypothesis: portfolio-fit or Not (tentative)

**Fail rule:** If you can't find real competitors advertising it, it doesn't pass R0.

## GATE R2 — DOSSIER

**Goal:** Decision-grade dossier answering: who buys it, why now, what makes us different, how we sell it without looking like a random dropship store.

**Required Sections (all must be complete):**

**R2-A) Route Decision**
- portfolio-fit: trust-first wellness/fitness/beauty umbrella, "tested not just listed" vibes
- not-portfolio-fit: desire-channeling microbrand (separate identity lane)

**R2-B) ICP Snapshot**
- Who they are (identity), Moment of purchase, Pain, Desired outcome, Top objections

**R2-C) Market Map (3-7 competitors)**
- Platform evidence, Price/offer, Main claims/angles, Funnel type, Proof used

**R2-D) Differentiation Plan (3 defensible wedges)**
Not "better quality" — examples: mechanism clarity, better offer architecture, better proof assets, better trust cluster

**R2-E) Offer Architecture + Bundling**
- Core offer (1 unit)
- 2-4 bundles (2-pack, 3-pack, kit, family set)
- 3-8 cross-sells (adjacent items, AOV increase)
- LP Trust Catalog Cluster: mini-collection that builds trust near LP/PDP, same job-to-be-done/body area/routine. Must NOT look like random general store.

**R2-F) Creative Intel**
- 10 hook angles (first 1-3 seconds)
- 5 repeatable creative patterns observed
- Compliance Do/Don't list (words/claims to avoid, visuals to avoid, safe framing alternatives)

**R2-G) Supply + White Label Feasibility (no samples)**
- Supplier scorecard (2-5), private-label capability checklist, risk notes

## GATE R3 — BUILD-READY PACKET

**Goal:** Store builder + lister can execute without asking any questions.

**Required contents:**
1. Executive Summary (max 6 bullets): what it is, who it's for, why it wins
2. Final Route Decision + update Notion Store Route field
3. Store-Builder Ready Spec: 3 title options, variant structure, PDP section order, copy blocks (hero, problem, solution, proof, FAQ, disclaimers), asset list (exact)
4. Offer + Bundling + Trust Cluster
5. Creative Intel: 10 hooks, 5 patterns, 5 "don't do this" warnings
6. Supplier + White Label recommendation
